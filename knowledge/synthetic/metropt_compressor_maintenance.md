# Synthetic MetroPT Compressor Maintenance Notes

## Scope

This synthetic note supports AegisAI retrieval experiments for the MetroPT compressor dataset. It is not official equipment documentation.

## Pressure path symptoms

Pressure path investigation should consider TP2, TP3, H1, reservoir pressure, air flow signals, and operating state transitions. A forecast deviation in TP2 or TP3 is evidence of pressure behavior that needs review, not proof of mechanical root cause.

## Lubrication and thermal symptoms

Oil temperature and oil level evidence should be reviewed with compressor state, motor current, and recent operating transitions. A memory of prior anomalies can support investigation but does not prove recurrence.

## Evidence

Capture timestamp, sensor, normalized evidence value, operating state, forecast horizon, and whether the signal is current anomaly, trend, or forecast deviation.
